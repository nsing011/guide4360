❓ FAQs
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Is it really free to deploy?
A: The chatbot itself is free. Google Gemini has a free tier (60 reqs/min).
   Pay-as-you-go is very cheap (~$0.0025 per 1K tokens).

Q: Do I need to change the database?
A: No! Zero schema changes needed. Works with your existing database.

Q: Is my data secure?
A: Yes! Users only see their own data. API key is server-side protected.

Q: How do I add this to my team?
A: Just click the "Assistant" button in the navigation. It's there!

Q: Can I modify the chatbot?
A: Yes! The code is fully open and documented. Easy to customize.

Q: What if I don't like it?
A: You can disable it easily. No dependencies on other parts of the app.

Q: Can I export conversations?
A: Currently conversations are session-only. Can add storage if needed.

Q: What's the roadmap?
A: Message history, analytics, integrations, and more planned!

See CHATBOT_FAQ.md for more questions and answers.
